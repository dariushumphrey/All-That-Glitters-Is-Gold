<size=48>Player</size>
- The Evasion invulnerability effect is now more visible.
- <color=yellow>[Implement]</color> The player-character's weapon anchor can now perform actions:
	- Firing pushes the anchor, simulating recoil.
	- Sprinting relocates the anchor ahead of the character.
	- Being idle relocates the anchor to the character's side.
	- Melee attacking or guarding rotates the anchor in a defensive motion.
- <color=cyan>[FIX]</color> Fixed an issue where Activator Drones spawned with the rotation of the weapon.  
- <color=cyan>[FIX]</color> Fixed an issue where Melee attacks remained locked in on enemies that died before the attack could connect. Fixed an issue where Melee attacks remained locked in on Combustible Lucent (Level 03) that disappeared before the attack could connect.

<size=48>Enemies</size>
- Enemies can now be staggered, recoiling backwards for 1 second before resuming their behavior (if their attack was blocked by a guard or if damaged by a staggering attack).
- <color=cyan>[FIX]</color> Fixed an issue where Replevin Ambuscade boss could stutter between waypoint navigation and player detection when tap-firing weapons.
- <color=cyan>[FIX]</color> Fixed a build issue where Replevin Ambuscade (Level 03) and Replevin Keystone (Level 05) bosses traversed waypoints randomly instead of a fixed order.
- Replevin Keystone boss has been updated:
	- Its new pounce attack variant is a short dash that transitions into a delayed AOE attack.
	- Can perform a third attack: Jettisons a Lucent shard from its back, detonating into 8 harmful Lucent clusters (up to 40 by Difficulty 5).
		- <color=cyan>[FIX]</color> This attack is inspired by a fix to an issue where evading past its old pounce attack variant would force an incorrect distance reading, stalling the Keystone from moving and forcing an endless discharge of harmful Lucent clusters unless the player moved far away or decided to jump into its attack ray.
	- Now regains 1/4 of its max Health when entering Phase II up to Difficulty 3. Starting from Difficulty 4, Keystone regains all missing health.
- <color=cyan>[FIX]</color> Fixed an issue where Boss enemy health canvases remained active while the game was paused.
